01 - Who I am

Just someone learning to build stuff.

I am a high school student who got into coding and kind of never stopped. I found Hack Club and it made a lot of sense to me. Building real things is way more interesting than doing exercises from a textbook.

This is one of my first websites. I built it with HTML and CSS, no frameworks, no templates. It took some time to figure out but that is kind of the point. You learn by doing, not by reading about it.

I am still learning. Most of what is on this site I figured out by trying things and seeing what broke. That is honestly how it works.

02 - What I am learning

Things I have picked up so far.

  • HTML structure and how pages are built
  • CSS styling, colors, fonts, and spacing
  • How to read documentation when I get stuck
  • Why naming things well actually matters
  • That broken code is normal and not a big deal
  • How to look at other sites and learn from them
