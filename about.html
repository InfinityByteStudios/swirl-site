01 - What is Hack Club

A community of student hackers.

Hack Club is a global network of high school hackers and coding clubs. Students learn to code by building real projects, not by doing exercises from textbooks. It is all about making things and sharing them with others.

Hack Club provides everything you need to start a coding club at your school. You get support, resources, and a community of thousands of other students who are building cool stuff. No experience required, just curiosity.

The community runs on Slack where students share projects, ask for help, and hang out. It is a place where making things is celebrated and everyone is welcome to join and learn together.

02 - What Hack Club offers

Resources for student hackers.

  • Free coding workshops and project guides
  • A global Slack community with thousands of students
  • Support for starting a club at your school
  • Hackathons and events around the world
  • Hardware grants and project funding
  • A place to share what you build and get feedback
